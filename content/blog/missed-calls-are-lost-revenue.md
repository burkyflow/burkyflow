Most service businesses underestimate how many calls they miss. When you pull the actual phone logs, the number is rarely a couple. It is often a third of inbound calls, and each one is a job that went to whoever picked up second.

## Why it happens

Crews are on jobs, the front desk is busy, and after hours nobody is there at all. The caller does not leave a voicemail. They simply dial the next business.

An AI voice receptionist closes that gap. It answers on the first ring, qualifies the caller, and books the work, day or night.

## What to do about it

1. Pull your call logs and count the real miss rate.
2. Put a number on the average job value.
3. Multiply. That is the revenue walking out the door every month.

Then automate the answer so no call goes unanswered again.
