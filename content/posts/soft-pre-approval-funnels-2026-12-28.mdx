The biggest reason a customer does not start a financing conversation is fear of the credit pull. They do not want an inquiry on their report for a car they may not buy, and they do not want to be told no in person. A soft pre-approval funnel removes both fears: a soft inquiry that does not affect the score, a result in minutes, and no commitment. For the dealer or lender it produces a pipeline of customers who have already shown their hand.

## What a soft pre-approval is, and is not

A soft pull returns enough of the customer's credit profile to estimate the tier they would qualify for, without recording a hard inquiry. It is a pre-qualification, not an approval. The final decision still needs a full application and a hard pull at the point of sale. The funnel should say so plainly: "This does not affect your credit score and is not a loan offer."

## Where the funnel lives

The funnel is a short form reachable from everywhere a customer might wonder "could I get financed?":

- A button on every vehicle listing page: "See your estimated payment."
- A link in reactivation and refinance messages: "Check your options in two minutes."
- A text keyword customers can send after seeing a sign in the service lane or on the lot.
- A link the AI receptionist can text to a caller who asks about financing.

The form asks for name, contact details, consent, and the minimum needed for the soft pull. The result page shows an estimated tier and a payment range, and offers the next step: book a visit, pick a vehicle, or talk to someone now.

## Moving pre-qualified customers to a deal

The pre-qualification is the start, not the result. The follow-up determines whether it becomes a funded deal:

1. **Instant handoff.** A pre-qualified customer is a hot lead. The 5-minute rule applies: a text within a minute confirming the result and asking which vehicle or which day, and a person within five.
2. **Matched inventory.** Show two or three vehicles that fit the estimated payment range, not the whole lot.
3. **Soft to hard, at the right time.** The hard pull happens when the customer has chosen a vehicle and is ready to apply, not before.
4. **Not-now handling.** Customers who pre-qualify and go quiet get one follow-up a week later, then a re-check at 30 days. Their tier may improve; rates may move.

A [CRM with routing built in](/services/crm-lead-systems) tracks every pre-qualification through those states, so none sits unworked.

## Using pre-approval in reactivation

Soft pre-approval is also the best reply mechanism for database campaigns. A refinance message that says "reply YES" produces a conversation. A refinance message with a pre-qualification link produces a number, and customers who see a lower payment in writing are far more likely to book. The same applies to lease-end, negative equity, and subprime rebuild messages: the link turns a vague offer into a specific one.

## What to measure

- Form starts and completions, by source
- Pre-qualification results by tier
- Time from completion to first human contact
- Appointment rate and funded-deal rate from pre-qualified customers
- Comparison: funded rate for pre-qualified leads versus standard internet leads

Pre-qualified leads should convert at a multiple of standard leads, because the financing objection is already handled.

## Compliance

Soft-pull pre-qualification is regulated: the customer must consent to the inquiry, the result must be described as an estimate rather than an offer, and the data must be handled under the applicable privacy and fair lending rules. Adverse outcomes have their own notice requirements once a real application is made. Build the funnel with the lender's compliance team and keep the language fixed.
