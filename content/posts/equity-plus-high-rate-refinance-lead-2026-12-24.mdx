Refinance lenders spend heavily to find customers who are paying too much on a car loan. Dealerships and captive lenders already know exactly who those customers are, because they wrote the loans. A customer with equity in the vehicle and a rate above today's market is a refinance lead. If you do not make the offer, a credit union will, and the customer leaves your portfolio.

## The two signals

A refinance makes sense when two things are true at once:

1. **Equity.** The vehicle is worth more than the payoff, or close to it. That lets a lender refinance at a sensible loan-to-value.
2. **Rate gap.** The customer's rate is meaningfully above what they would qualify for today, because rates fell, their credit improved, or both.

Either signal alone is a maybe. Together they are a lead with a real benefit: a lower payment, a shorter term, or both.

## Finding them

Every input is in the loan file:

- Original rate and term
- Payoff balance
- Vehicle value, adjusted for mileage
- Payment history since origination
- Original credit tier

Run the comparison monthly. Customers who cross both thresholds, equity above your minimum and rate gap above your minimum, with 12 or more clean payments, form the refinance-ready segment. A [CRM connected to the loan data](/services/crm-lead-systems) can maintain that segment without anyone pulling reports.

## The offer

Keep it about the payment, because that is what the customer feels:

> Hi [Name], you financed your [Year Model] with us in [year]. Based on your payment history and today's rates, you may qualify for a lower payment. Want us to run the numbers? Reply YES.

The reply goes to a finance manager with the current loan and an estimated new payment already calculated. Soft pre-qualification confirms the number before the customer commits to anything.

## Why keeping the refinance matters

When a customer refinances elsewhere, you lose more than the interest:

- The relationship moves to the new lender, who will market the next vehicle.
- Service contracts and GAP tied to the old loan may be cancelled.
- The customer's next purchase conversation starts somewhere else.

Offering the refinance yourself keeps the customer, the products, and the next deal.

## Combine with the service drive

Refinance-ready customers who are also service customers can hear the offer in person. The advisor sees the flag at write-up: "While you're here, our finance team found you may qualify for a lower payment. Want them to show you?" The appointment is already happening.

## What to measure

- Customers in the refinance-ready segment each month
- Contact and reply rates
- Refinances completed, average payment reduction
- Portfolio retention: share of eligible customers who refinanced with you versus elsewhere

## Compliance

"May qualify" is the right language; approval depends on underwriting. Text consent and an opt-out are required, and the rate and payment examples must reflect what a real customer in that segment can get.
