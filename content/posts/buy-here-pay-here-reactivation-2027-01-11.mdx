A buy-here-pay-here dealer knows its customers better than almost any other business in automotive. Every payment, every late notice, every vehicle, and every phone number is in the system, often going back years. Yet most BHPH marketing is aimed at strangers: signs, radio, and "no credit needed" ads. The customers who paid off a loan with you, the ones who are halfway through one, and the ones who left are a more reliable source of the next sale, and they cost nothing to reach.

## The segments already in your system

**Paid-off customers.** They finished the loan. They proved they can pay. Their vehicle is now several years older, and they have no reason to go elsewhere unless nobody asks. This is the single best segment in BHPH.

**Mid-loan customers in good standing.** Twelve or more on-time payments. They may qualify for a newer vehicle at a similar payment, or for a better rate on the current one. They are also your best referral source.

**Customers approaching payoff.** Three to six months out. The next-vehicle conversation should start now, not after the title is released.

**Past customers who left.** Traded elsewhere, or sold the car. Some left for reasons you can fix. Service and parts are a way back in.

**Referral sources.** BHPH customers refer family and friends constantly. Formalize it: a thank-you and a referral offer at payoff and at the 12-month mark.

## The messages

Each segment gets one specific message:

> Hi [Name], congratulations on paying off your [Year Model]. You've built a strong record with us. If you ever want to look at something newer at a similar payment, we'd like to make it easy. Reply YES and we'll set up a time.

> Hi [Name], you're 12 payments in and in great standing. That may qualify you for a lower payment or a newer vehicle. Want a quick review? Reply YES.

> Hi [Name], your loan with us wraps up in a few months. Before then, want to see what the next step could look like? Reply YES.

Plain, respectful, and about the customer's record. BHPH customers are used to being treated as risks; being treated as proven customers is memorable.

## Handling the reply

Replies should reach a salesperson or the finance desk within minutes, with the customer's payment history on screen. After hours, an [AI receptionist](/services/ai-voice-receptionist) can confirm interest, answer basic questions about payment ranges, and book a visit. The customer should never be asked for information you already have.

## Why this beats "no credit needed" advertising

- **Known risk.** A customer with 24 on-time payments is a better underwriting decision than any walk-in.
- **No acquisition cost.** The list is yours.
- **Higher trust.** They have already been through your process once.
- **Lower default rates** on repeat customers, in most dealers' experience, because the relationship has history.

Reactivation does not replace advertising. It gives the store a base of repeat business that does not depend on it.

## Building it

A [CRM connected to the loan servicing data](/services/crm-lead-systems) segments customers by payment history, loan age, and payoff date automatically, and sends the right message at the right point. Replies and outcomes write back, so the next conversation starts informed. The same engine runs the delinquency-prevention reminders described later in this series.

## What to measure

- Customers in each segment, and share contacted per month
- Reply and appointment rates by segment
- Repeat deals, and their default rate compared with new customers
- Referrals received from each segment
- Share of total sales that are repeat or referral

## Compliance

Loan-related texts require consent and an opt-out. Messages to customers in collections fall under the FDCPA where it applies, and must be kept separate from marketing. Marketing messages should not reference delinquency. Keep the two paths distinct in the system.
