# Keyword targets

Search phrases worth targeting on burkyflow.com, with where each one is (or should be) used. Volumes are US monthly searches from OpenSEO (Nov 2026); difficulty is 0–100, lower is easier. "GSC" rows are from Search Console for May–Nov 2026: impressions and the average position the site already holds.

Rules: one primary phrase per page, used in the search title, the H1 or first paragraph, and the description. Secondary phrases go in H2s and body text where they read naturally. Do not stuff. See CLAUDE.md for title length and writing rules.

## Not worth targeting

- "receptionist", "receptionist job description", "front desk receptionist": job seekers, not buyers.
- Competitor and brand names that appear in Search Console (RingCentral, Smith.ai, "fixyflow", "recruitiflow"): accidental impressions.
- "humanize ai", "ai assistant": unrelated intent.
